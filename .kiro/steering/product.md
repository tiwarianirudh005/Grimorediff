# Product Steering: GrimoireDiff

## Goal
Build a codebase and `package.json` health auditor with a gothic telemetry UI ("Codebase Séance & Tombstone Explorer") that highlights risky/unused dependencies and unreferenced files while keeping text sharp, legible, and responsive.

## Design Rules
- High contrast and clear typography come first; spooky visuals (gravestones, spectral threads, ember/ectoplasm accents) must never obscure readability.
